LEONIO GROUP ERP - Prototype v0.6 (Business Central aligned: warehouse, treasury, intercompany, foreign currency)
========================================================================

HOW TO START
1. Double-click "Start ERP.bat".
2. A black window opens (the server) and your browser opens the ERP.
   Keep the black window open while you use the ERP. Close it to stop.
   If the browser does not open, go to http://localhost:8080
   The first start takes a few seconds (it loads your master data).

WHAT IS INSIDE
- Your Business Central master files (Land, Mining, Tankering) are already
  loaded: chart of accounts, posting groups, VAT/WHT setup, vendors,
  customers, items, locations, bins, dimensions, number series, fixed assets.
- Postings work like Business Central: every G/L account comes from the
  posting setup (General Posting Setup, Inventory/Vendor/Customer/Bank/FA
  Posting Groups, VAT Posting Setup, WHT Posting Setup).
- LLRI and LAMI share the Mining setup. Admin also uses Mining for now
  (no Admin master file was provided).

WHERE IS MY DATA?
- "data\md.json"  = master data (large, saved only when masters change)
- "data\db.json"  = transactions and settings
- Hourly backups in "data\backups". Copy the whole folder to keep your work.

FIRST STEPS
- "Getting Started" (left menu): a full purchase, step by step.
- "ERP Project > Flow <-> BC Reconciliation": each signed To-Be step, the
  Business Central function it uses, and how this ERP does it.
- "Administration > Master Data Check": problems found in the master files
  (things BC would also refuse at posting time).
- "Administration > Import BC Configuration Packages": re-load updated
  BC Excel files at any time.

IMPORTANT
This is a prototype for validating processes with the departments.
It has no passwords yet. Do not use it for official books until the items
on the Roadmap page under "Go-live readiness" are done.
