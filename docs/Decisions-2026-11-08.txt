LEONIO ERP DECISIONS (decided by the project owner, 8 Nov 2026)

DIRECTION
1. Company ERP: Our ERP + ERS
2. Luis's LG-ERP: design reference; pause new P2P building
3. ERS: becomes the requisition module of the ERP
4. Look: ERS look on every screen; compact tables for accounting
5. Item pictures: category icons for all items, plus photo upload where useful
6. First company to go live: LAMI (mine site)

PROCURE-TO-PAY
7. Purchase Invoice approval: 2 levels, plus a 3rd above PHP 500,000
   Note: there must be a facility to update the approvers, per amount or per hierarchy
8. PO approval: SC Manager, then SC VP; CLG approves electronically
9. Canvass/RFQ: 3 quotes above PHP 50,000; technical review for equipment and spare parts
10. Vendor accreditation: required before a vendor can receive a PO, with document expiry reminders
11. Vendor/customer bank details and payment terms changes: Supply Chain and its heads update;
    Accounting Shared Services checks; Treasury does final checking and approves
12. Advances to suppliers: keep the separate "Advances to Supplier" account
13. Purchase returns after 7 days: warn but allow with a reason

INVENTORY
14. Material issuance (MRF/MIF): through ERS in the ERP; stop the Excel tracker
15. Fuel: issue per equipment, daily tank reading, reorder alert at 80,000 L; sales to LLRI/LAMI as intercompany
16. Non-commercial (TOY) ore: track quantity at zero cost
17. Ore extraction costing: keep the Purchase Invoice method for now
18. Lambert/Alabang to mine: Transfer Orders; CPD-SCW-003-02 is the controlling document

ORDER-TO-CASH
19. NOSPA in the ERP: price, grade, volume, laycan, shipment no., deposit schedule
20. Deposit 1, Deposit 2, Provisional: prepayment invoices generated from the contract, approved in the ERP
21. 50 WMT limit: do not copy unless shown to be a real rule
22. Scrap/obsolete/asset sales: always a sales invoice; asset sales as fixed asset disposal

CASH MANAGEMENT
23. Daily Cash Position report in the ERP replaces the Excel file
24. Import CSV bank statements and match automatically
25. Official receipts/invoices from the ERP: not until BIR registration is done

BUILD ORDER
26. ERS + Material Issue -> Sales Contract and deposits -> Vendor accreditation and bank-change approval
    -> RFQ -> Service acceptance -> Cash Position and bank import -> Logins and shared database
