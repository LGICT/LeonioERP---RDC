Reports (ERS has 7 report tabs)

ERS reports share one filter bar (date range, department, status), show only what your role may see, and export to Excel/CSV. The ERP's reports are accounting reports (trial balance, financial statements, aging, inventory valuation); it has no requisition or approval reports yet.

Don't copy from ERS

  • One stock figure per item. The ERP keeps stock per location and bin, as Business Central does.
  • Stock that just goes down on release. The ERP posts the issue to inventory and the G/L at cost, charged to department and equipment.
  • Budget at catalog price by category. The ERP measures against real G/L actuals per account.
  • Data kept in one browser, and anyone can switch users. Both systems need real logins and a shared database.
  • A single Standard workflow for every request. The ERP already routes by superior, department head and amount.
  • Free-text "non-catalog" lines. They can't post to inventory or the right account; a new-item request to Supply Chain is safer.
